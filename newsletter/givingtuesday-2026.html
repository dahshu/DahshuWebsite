Dear friends,

In Chinese, DahShu means “big tree,” and it stands for community: a place where people gather under one wide canopy and form strong, lasting bonds. (It also sounds like the Chinese for “big data.”) We’re a 501(c)(3) non-profit with a simple mission: creating a global community for people to learn, exchange ideas, and share opportunities in data science. A tree only thrives when it is cared for, and this GivingTuesday, we need everyone’s help to keep our community growing strong.

Membership is free, and the community now includes more than 6,000 statisticians, data scientists, students, and professionals in the U.S. and around the world. Volunteers run everything we do, and we keep administrative costs under 3%, so nearly every dollar you give goes straight into programs.

What your gift sustains

Volunteers from the community build and run every one of these programs.

Free Monthly Virtual Journal Club

Every month, leading experts present cutting-edge data science research on Zoom, and the slides and recordings are free to everyone. This year’s talks covered generative AI for clinical evidence, digital twins, trustworthy AI, causal inference with real-world data, and Bayesian trial design, giving students and working professionals direct access to the field’s newest ideas.
 

Volunteering & career growth

Members gain leadership experience by organizing events. Since 2018, DahShu has been an approved certifying organization for the President’s Volunteer Service Award, and we also offer training sessions and career development tips.
 

A place to connect

Through our forums, bilingual English and Chinese channels, and conferences dating back to 2016, members find collaborators, mentors, and opportunities.
